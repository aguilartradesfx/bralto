Identity

The logo

Our mark pairs a location pin with a person — the promise of putting the right people exactly where they’re needed. Three approved variants, each for a specific background.

Usage rules

  • Always keep the logo optically aligned — never stretched, rotated or skewed.
  • Use only the three approved variants. Don’t recolor or apply gradients to the mark.
  • Never place the white logo on a light background, or the dark logo on a dark one.
  • No hard drop shadows, outlines or effects on the logo.
24px min. height · digital
12mm min. height · print

Clear space

Keep a minimum clear space of X on every side, where X equals the height of the logotype’s initial. Nothing should intrude into this margin.

Incorrect usage

Four things to never do with the mark.
